About the Club

Built by outdoor lovers, for outdoor lovers

Idaho Alpine Club brings together people who love hiking, climbing, backpacking, paddling, skiing, conservation, and exploring Idaho’s outdoor spaces. Every trip and stewardship day is organized by members.

  • Year-round trips for all skill levels
  • Mentorship from experienced members
  • Conservation and trail maintenance days
  • A friendly community based in Idaho Falls
